Privacy Policy

Welcome to Bridgr. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Bridgr mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our library management services, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Library information
  • Student records managed by library owners
  • Seat allocation information
  • Fee and subscription records

We may also collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, and diagnostic information to improve the performance and security of our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain Bridgr services.
  • To manage library operations.
  • To manage subscriptions and billing.
  • To improve application performance.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Payment Information

Subscription payments are securely processed through Razorpay.

Bridgr does not intentionally store sensitive payment information such as:

  • Credit card numbers
  • Debit card numbers
  • CVV
  • UPI PIN
  • Net banking credentials

Payment processing is handled by Razorpay in accordance with their own privacy and security practices.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your information is securely processed and stored using Bridgr's backend infrastructure and trusted hosting providers used to operate our services.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary:

  • To process payments through Razorpay.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect our legal rights and platform security.
  • With trusted service providers assisting in operating Bridgr under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Bridgr is intended for use by library owners and authorized users. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age required by applicable law.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with the revised "Last Updated" date.
